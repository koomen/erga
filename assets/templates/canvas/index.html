One big canvas

Every idea in this talk sits on one large canvas. Each step brings one frame into view.

Glide to the next

To go to the next frame, the camera glides across the canvas in one smooth move.

Make it yours

Type over the text in the editor. To move a frame, change its left and top.

Add a frame

Copy a section with the class frame. It becomes a step of its own, named by its heading.
