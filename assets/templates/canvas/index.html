One big canvas

Every idea in this talk sits on one large canvas. Each step zooms in on one frame.

Zoom out, zoom in

To go to the next frame, the camera pulls back to show the whole canvas, then flies in.

Make it yours

Type over the text in the editor. To move a frame, change its left and top.

Add a frame

Copy a section with the class frame. It becomes a step of its own, named by its heading.
