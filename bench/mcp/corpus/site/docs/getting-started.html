Getting started

This guide takes you from a new account to your first paid invoice in about 10 minutes.

1. Create your account

Sign up with your work email. Your trial is the Starter plan ($12 per month after 14 days), with up to 3 users.

2. Add your business details

Add your studio's name, address, tax number and logo under Settings. They appear on every invoice.

3. Send your first invoice

  1. Choose New invoice and pick a client, or add one.
  2. Add the lines: a description, a quantity and a rate.
  3. Choose Send. The client gets an email with a payment link.

4. Turn on reminders

Under Reminders, choose when Ledgerly writes to clients who have not paid. Most studios keep the default of 3, 10 and 30 days.
