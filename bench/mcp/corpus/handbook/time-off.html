Time off

Rest is part of the work. We would rather you took all your time off than none of it.

Vacation

Full-time staff get 20 days of paid vacation each calendar year, on top of public holidays. Book them in the HR tool at least 2 weeks ahead for anything longer than 3 days.

Part-time allowances are pro rata: someone who works 4 days a week gets 16 days, and someone who works 3 days a week gets 12 days.

You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Use them by the end of March.

Sick days

If you are ill, stop working and tell your manager. You do not need a note for the first 7 days. See Benefits for health cover.
