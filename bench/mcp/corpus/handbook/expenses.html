Expenses

If you spend money for work, Fernhill pays you back. Use common sense: spend as if it were your own money.

How to claim

  1. Take a photo of the receipt.
  2. Add the claim in the HR tool, with the receipt and one line on what it was for.
  3. Submit claims within 20 days of the purchase. We pay them with the next salary.
