Your first weeks

Your manager and an onboarding buddy look after you for your first month. Your buddy is someone from another team, so you meet more people.

Week 1

  1. Collect your laptop, or receive it by courier before your first day.
  2. Set up your accounts with IT on your first morning: email, chat, the code host and the HR tool.
  3. Have a 30-minute call with each person on your team.
  4. Add your 20 vacation days to the HR tool's planner, even if you have no dates yet, so you can see them.

Weeks 2 to 4

  • Ship something small to customers, with your buddy's help.
  • Sit in on 3 support calls with clinics, to hear how they use the product.
  • Read How we work and tell your manager what surprised you.

Equipment

You can spend up to £500 on a desk, chair or screen for home in your first 3 months. Claim it as described in Expenses.
