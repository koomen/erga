How we work

Planning

We plan in 6-week cycles, with 2 weeks between cycles for fixes, tidying and time to think. Each team picks its own projects for a cycle from the company's list of problems.

Meetings

  • Each team's stand-up is 20 minutes at most, three times a week.
  • The company meets on Fridays at 15:00 for 45 minutes: demos and news.
  • One-to-ones with your manager are every 2 weeks, for 30 minutes.

Writing things down

Decisions go in writing, in the project's document, not only in chat. If you were not in a meeting, you should be able to read what it decided within a day.

Shipping

We release to customers every weekday before 14:00, so that people are around to fix problems. Nobody releases on a Friday afternoon or before time off.
