Welcome to Fernhill

Fernhill makes scheduling software for veterinary clinics. We are 38 people, in Bristol and remote across the UK. This handbook says how we work together. If something in it is wrong or out of date, change it: everyone can edit it.

Where to start

  • New here? Read Your first weeks first.
  • How we plan, meet and ship is in How we work.
  • Holidays, sick days and leave are in Time off.
  • Pension, health cover and equipment are in Benefits.

The short version

  • Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 UK time. Outside them, work when it suits you.
  • Everyone gets 20 days of paid vacation a year, plus the 8 UK public holidays.
  • We meet in person in Bristol once a quarter, for 2 days. Travel is paid.
  • Ask in the #help channel when you are stuck. Nobody minds.
