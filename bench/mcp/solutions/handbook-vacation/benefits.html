Benefits

Time off

25 days' paid vacation, the 8 public holidays, and 20 weeks of parental leave at full pay. The details are in Time off.

Pension

Fernhill pays 6% of your salary into your pension, and you choose how much to add. You can change it once a month in the HR tool.

Health

  • Private health cover for you and your family, from your first day.
  • An eye test every 2 years, and up to £150 towards screen glasses.
  • Free, private counselling: up to 8 sessions a year.

Learning

Each person has £1,000 a year for books, courses and conferences. Claim it like other costs, as described in Expenses.
