About us

We started Ledgerly in 2019 after a year of chasing our own clients for money.

Our story

Ana and Joel ran a four-person design studio in Lisbon. In their best year, they spent more evenings on unpaid invoices than on design. The tools they tried were made for accountants, not for the people doing the work.

They built the first version of Ledgerly for their own studio, then for five friends' studios. Today 6,000 studios in 31 countries send their invoices with it.

How we work

We are a team of 24, fully remote, across nine time zones. We answer every support email within one working day, and the people who build Ledgerly take turns answering them.
