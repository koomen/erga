Year in review

Where we are

Northwind sold 3,400 cargo bikes this year, up 41%, from two shops and the web store.

Who buys

Customers

Two in three buyers are parents who replaced a second car. Most live within 5 km of a shop.

Outside view

Market

Cargo bike sales in our three cities grow about 25% a year, and city subsidies end in 2028.

Money in

Pricing

Our bikes sell at a premium of 15% over the nearest rival. Service plans add a margin we do not use yet.

What we build

Roadmap

A folding model in spring, a rental fleet for families in summer, and a third shop in the autumn.

Money out

Budget

Spend grows from 2.1 to 2.9 million: two thirds on the new shop and the rental fleet.

People

Hiring

Four mechanics, one fleet manager and a second buyer. The mechanics are the hardest roles to fill.

Watch out

Risks

Battery supply, the end of city subsidies, and theft of bikes from the rental fleet.

Decisions

Next steps

Sign the lease for the third shop by March, and order the rental fleet before the price rise.
