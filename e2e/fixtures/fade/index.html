The harbour at dawn

Paragraph 1. The boats come in one at a time, low in the water, and the people on the quay count them without a word. Each has its own sound: a cough of the engine, a rattle of chain, a voice that calls a name across the water. Nobody hurries. The day is long, and the sea gives it its shape.
